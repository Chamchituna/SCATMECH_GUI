SCATMECH: Polarized Light Scattering C++ Class Library

Upstream: https://github.com/usnistgov/SCATMECH
Documentation: https://pages.nist.gov/SCATMECH/

Local integration
-----------------

BRDFProg.cpp and MieProg.cpp include the existing SCATMECH GUI adaptations
described in README.md. geometry_export.cpp is the SCATMECH GUI native
geometry exporter. These additions are separate from the upstream NIST
library; their distribution terms follow the SCATMECH GUI project.
